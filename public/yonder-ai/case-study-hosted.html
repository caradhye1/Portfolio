01 · The challenge

Building a Zero to One product in a fast-paced world of social media and AI

Our clients were big brands like McDonalds, The Home Depot, Walmart, and Disney, to name a few. Current client needs were crucial for revenue at a small company.

Product updates had to be shipped quickly and in a way that clients would appreciate.

User feedback told us the previous insights were too text-heavy and difficult to interpret actionably for our audience.

My work involved

  1. Understanding the ML-generated info

  2. Compressing it into easily understandable insight

  3. Visualizing that insight and designing a feature in the product

  4. Working with Engg to work around any technical issues with databases, frameworks etc.

02 · The idea

4 basic questions

I narrowed down much of the info we were generating into 4 basic questions based on user interviews.

Try it · pick a questionSignals are the prototype’s sample data for one narrative
  1. 1A question is already in the analyst’s headA tab called Authenticity asks them to translate. A tab called “Should I care?” is the sentence they arrived with, so the label does the navigation.
  2. 2Selecting a question shows its evidencePick one and the signals behind it light up. Fourteen signals stop being a wall, and every answer can be traced to what produced it.
  3. 3The fourth question has no signalNo model output answers “What can I do?”. That is where the product hands over to a person, so it gets actions and no chart.

QAnon

Adversarial

QAnon believers are far-right, fringe conspiracy theorists. They follow Q, an anonymous influencer claiming to be a top government official.

QAnon has low influence on the narratives it joins, at roughly 22% of the scale. Its activity lands with low impact on the general public, the lowest of three levels. Its accounts sit on the far right of a five-step political spectrum that runs from far left to far right. Around 13% of its accounts appear inauthentic. It posts most on 8kun, which accounts for 40% of its activity, followed by 4chan at 30%, and reddit and Twitter at 13% each.

07 · Reflection

Reflection

Today I would change a lot of things about the way I designed the product. Mainly around UI consistency, iconography, etc.

We had to make several compromises due to the speed needs of the project.

The prototype on this page, Yonder AI 2.0, rebuilds the product on one design system: three tiers of tokens, one icon grid and stroke weight, and hover, focus, pressed and disabled states on every control.
